## What It Does

AI voice agents for real estate. Answers inbound calls 24/7, makes outbound calls at scale. Core problem: 60% of RE calls go unanswered — lost leads, poor tenant experience.

**Core capabilities:**
- 24/7 inbound call answering
- Outbound batch calling campaigns
- Call transfer (warm w/ summary or cold)
- Recording, transcription, SMS/email summaries
- Spam screening
- Emergency maintenance triage
- Lead qualification & capture
- Appointment/inspection scheduling
- Property listing sync (Domain.com.au + realestate.com.au)
- CRM updates, contact creation
- Custom function calls to external APIs mid-call
- Webhook integrations
- Configurable guardrails (pricing limits, escalation rules)

**Channels:** Voice (live), SMS (coming soon), Email (coming soon)
